# Employee Attrition Analysis – EDA

## Project Overview

This project focuses on exploratory data analysis (EDA) of employee attrition using Python.

The objective is to identify the main factors associated with employee turnover and extract meaningful insights from the dataset.

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about employees, including:

- Age
- Job Satisfaction
- Monthly Income
- Overtime
- Job Role
- Years at Company
- Attrition

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Project Steps

1. Data loading
2. Dataset overview
3. Data quality checking
4. Data cleaning
5. Exploratory data analysis
6. Data visualization
7. Key insights
8. Conclusion

## Visualizations

The project includes visualizations analyzing:

- Attrition distribution
- Age and attrition
- Overtime and attrition
- Monthly income and attrition
- Job role and attrition
- Job satisfaction and attrition
- Years at company and attrition

## Key Findings

The analysis shows that employee attrition is associated with several factors.

Employees working overtime have a higher attrition rate than employees who do not work overtime.

Lower job satisfaction is also associated with a higher attrition rate.

Employees who leave the company have, on average, lower monthly income, are younger, and have fewer years at the company compared with employees who stay.

These findings represent associations observed in the dataset and do not prove causation.

## Conclusion

This exploratory analysis provides insights into employee attrition and can help identify factors that may be associated with employee turnover.

The project demonstrates the use of Python and data analysis libraries to clean, explore, visualize, and interpret a real-world HR dataset.
